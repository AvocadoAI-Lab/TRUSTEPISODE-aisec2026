\begin{figure*}[t]

The affine calibrator maps the stateless raw logit to $P_\epsilon$ only within a manifest-supported telemetry regime, while the deterministic sufficiency evaluator independently derives the multi-axis profile $U_\epsilon$. The sufficiency profile records evidence conditions but does not modify or relabel $P_\epsilon$.}

\begin{figure*}[t]

\begin{figure*}[t]

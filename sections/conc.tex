\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\trustepisode defines a narrow, replayable contract for EDR/NDR episode formation and
candidate-conditioned maliciousness calibration. Episode Synthesis alone owns immutable
membership revisions; the scorer, support resolver, calibrator, sufficiency evaluator, and
AuditRecord Assembler have disjoint outputs; each revision terminates in either a complete
AuditRecord or an explicit AssemblyFailureRecord; and missing or incompatible telemetry yields an
explicit out-of-support state rather than a fabricated probability. The primary evaluation
executes frozen CALDERA attack families on isolated live Ubuntu targets and pairs each execution
with replay perturbations or authenticated collector outages. Performance deltas are interpreted
together with the five sufficiency-state transitions, so honest abstention under missing evidence
is distinguished from silent confidence. These CALDERA and robustness results remain pending;
the current draft therefore makes no claim of deployment generalization.

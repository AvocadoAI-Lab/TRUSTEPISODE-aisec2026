\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\trustepisode is a deterministic, auditable EDR/NDR reconstruction contract and a read-only
evidence boundary for AI-assisted investigation:
single-owner immutable revisions, append-only terminal outcomes with a complete failure-code
matrix, and stage-localized A--G evidence-loss diagnosis. On the sealed M1--M7 cohort, sliding
sessionization improves completeness relative to fixed bins, but fair sliding time/entity/relation
builders do not establish TrustEpisode superiority. EB4 matches EB2 on all 70 action-coverage
pairs, but trails EB1/EB3 on one concurrent-benign M7 run; the EB4--EB1 action-coverage estimate
is $\Delta=\EbOneActionDelta{}$ with 95\% CI
$[\EbOneActionCILo{},\EbOneActionCIHi{}]$. That mostly-overlapping result is forensic evidence
about benchmark coverage and one boundary-case cost: advanced guards require targeted
conformance cards, which we provide without treating them as live effectiveness. Canonical
replay is byte-identical on all 60 sealed malicious runs; raw arrival-order robustness requires
re-canonicalization. M2's missing \texttt{file\_transfer} token is
\texttt{D\_token\_mapping\_failure}, not a demonstrated formation drop. Contract ablation shows
which properties prevent mutable history, silent missing-input failure, and noncanonical replay.
Eight downstream-boundary cards accept the valid canonical control, reject six schema-invalid or
unsealed mutations, and accept a resealed allowed-field negative control. The latter shows why an
unkeyed digest is not writer authentication; deployed authorization remains unevaluated.
A separate once-run held-out Qwen2.5-7B pilot yields
\LlmPilotAnswers{}/\LlmPilotTotal{} exact answers but only
\LlmPilotExact{}/\LlmPilotTotal{} fully exact answer/abstention/citation triples. A same-card
replication, locked after that result but before querying deployment-default Gemma3-1B, yields
\GemmaPilotAnswers{}/20 exact answers, no exact citation sets, no fully exact responses, and
\GemmaPilotInvalidRefs{} invalid pointers. This is evidence that a typed record makes
model-dependent failure inspectable, not that either model is generally accurate or that
the observed gap estimates a size effect.
Supplemental partition artifacts (not primary estimators) include L1 live bases with offline
L1/L2 ablations, L3 controlled hub inject, L4 controlled late inject, wall-clock within-horizon
holds (2/2), and sealed beyond-horizon attempts without organic LateEvidenceRecord. Sealed M7 is
a primary 3900\,s boundary-stress family with expected zero recovery beyond $W_{max}$. Fitted scoring, calibration, and
telemetry-outage robustness remain unevaluated and outside these claims. A deployed read-only
trace confirms that source-separated Suricata/Wazuh evidence and ATT\&CK references reach the
analyst case view; its displayed confidence is not treated as the contract's probability or as
LLM-effectiveness evidence. The result is a falsifiable evidence substrate for future AI
reasoning; both negative audits reinforce rather than remove the need to distrust the model.

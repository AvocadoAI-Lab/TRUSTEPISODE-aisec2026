\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Alert correlation, provenance, and revision semantics.}
Classical alert-correlation methods connect observations into attack scenarios using temporal,
causal, and prerequisite relations~\cite{ning2002attackscenarios}. Provenance-based intrusion
detection preserves process and information-flow context; HOLMES, NODLINK, and KAIROS show how
causal graphs support multi-stage reconstruction and investigation
~\cite{holmes2019,nodlink2024,kairos2024}. CAPTAIN further demonstrates that a lightweight,
interpretable rule system can adapt deployment-specific parameters~\cite{captain2025}. HOLMES,
NODLINK, KAIROS, and CAPTAIN are also the Tier-Public comparators in
Section~\ref{sec:results}, where reported output grains stay explicit rather than being forced
into one metric. Separately, stream-processing research formalizes event time, watermarks, and
out-of-order records~\cite{akidau2015dataflow}, but does not define security-episode membership or
revision-scoped probability semantics. These lines of work motivate individual components of
\trustepisode; our focus is the combined EDR/NDR contract for deterministic candidate
construction, single-owner immutable revision semantics, manifest-scoped calibration, and
replay, rather than a new provenance-graph detector.

\paragraph{Calibration, support, and incomplete telemetry.}
Ranking and probability calibration answer different questions: strong discrimination does not
imply reliable probabilities~\cite{guo2017calibration,gneiting2007proper}. Cybersecurity-specific
calibration research likewise treats arbitrary threat scores as inputs that must be mapped and
evaluated as probabilities~\cite{threatcalibration2023}. Platt-style affine calibration provides
a compact monotone mapping~\cite{platt1999probabilistic}, while stable reliability diagrams and
proper-score decomposition support reproducible calibration diagnostics
~\cite{reliabilitydiag2021}. More broadly, missing-modality research studies predictive behavior
when inputs are absent~\cite{missingmodality2024}, and network-telemetry work shows that
abstraction can remove evidence required for forensic inference
~\cite{vaseghipanah2026blindspots}. These studies motivate explicit treatment of missing or
unsupported evidence, but they do not by themselves define \trustepisode's detector-health and
support contract, in which the raw scorer reads neither health nor labels and out-of-support
revisions keep a diagnostic logit while their probability field stays null
(Section~\ref{sec:method}).

\paragraph{Scope.}
The present method does not estimate enterprise-wide risk, operational impact, or response
priority, and does not use CTI, RAG/agents, Identity as an online source, autonomous response, or
OT/ICS-specific generalization. Its claims are limited to deterministic EDR/NDR episode
formation and revision, manifest-scoped candidate-conditioned maliciousness calibration,
structured evidence sufficiency, and replayable audit records.

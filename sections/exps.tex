\subsection{Evidence Classes and Cohorts}
\label{sec:research-questions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{RQ1} reproducibility --- sealed offline replay on all 60 primary malicious runs.

        seven failure codes + late/beyond-horizon cards.
  \item \textbf{RQ3} evidence-loss localization --- A--G taxonomy conformance + sealed M2 waterfall.
  \item \textbf{RQ4} mechanism coverage --- live M1--M6 + synthetic T1--T8; M7 only as
        supplemental boundary stress.
  \item \textbf{RQ5} baseline interpretation --- sealed fair EB0--EB4 comparison on M1--M6.

(ii)~sealed offline replay on those primary seals; (iii)~synthetic conformance (A1--A12, T1--T8,
AB0--AB3, A--G fixtures); (iv)~unit/integration boundary fixtures; (v)~supplemental partition
artifacts (L1--L4, wall-clock, sealed M7) that are \emph{not} mixed into primary estimators.
Synthetic cards are never mixed into primary live effectiveness. Sealed
Table~\ref{tab:table4-amended-results} primary cells were not overwritten.

\subsection{Ground Truth and Matching}
\label{sec:ground-truth}

Offline matching uses temporal IoU $\ge0.10$, entity Jaccard $\ge0.50$, overlapping required
action tokens, and weight
\begin{equation}
w=0.5A_{cov}+0.3J_{entity}+0.2\operatorname{IoU}_{time};
\quad w\ge\theta_{match}=0.50.
\label{eq:match-weight}
\end{equation}
Exact-complete recovery requires $A_{cov}=1$. Online builders never read evaluation fields.

Under fair sliding baselines, EB1--EB4 are identical on every primary metric and every family:
paired EB4$-$EB1 differences are zero with 60 ties and CIs $[0,0]$. We report \emph{no observed
difference} (not a formal equivalence test). Relation-aware formation is \emph{not} reported as
better than fair sliding-time.

\paragraph{Why sliding-time already suffices on M1--M6.}
The sealed primary cohort does not make advanced relations the unique linkage path: time and
entity sessionization already recover the same matched episodes. Hub, parent-merge, and late
guards therefore show no end-to-end metric delta. End-to-end F1 alone can misleadingly suggest
that complex policy is ``validated'' when the benchmark never stresses it. Future BAS cohorts
should register mechanism-coverage requirements (session-only, endpoint-only, hub over-merge,
late horizon, beyond-$W_{max}$ span) before claiming relation-aware gains.

\subsection{Supplemental Partition (Not Primary)}
\label{sec:supplemental-live}

Artifacts below live under a separate \texttt{supplemental\_mechanism} partition (or sealed M7
membership). They do \emph{not} enter Table~\ref{tab:table4-amended-results} or the 60-run fair
baseline estimator. Evidence classes are stated per row.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{L1 (live base + offline ablation):} three sealed live M3 SSH cross-host bases;
        offline ablation links under \texttt{authenticated\_session} alone and fragments when
        that relation is disabled (3/3). Not a claim of organic session-only BAS stress.
  \item \textbf{L2 (offline ablation on L1 seals):} on the same live SSH seals,
        \texttt{endpoint\_connection} alone (entity/session/causal disabled) links denser than
        ablating endpoint (3/3). Offline-only mechanism check.
  \item \textbf{L3 (offline ablation + controlled inject):} organic hub load is \emph{not}
        observed (hub-on vs hub-off identical, 0/3 organic). Controlled hub-load inject on the
        live sealed bases (cutoff$+1$ peers) makes hub-on vs hub-off diverge (3/3). Controlled
        inject $\neq$ organic BAS hub stress.
  \item \textbf{L4 late evidence (two distinct classes):}
        (i)~\emph{controlled late inject} on live seals yields successors and
        \texttt{beyond\_revision\_horizon} LateEvidenceRecords (3/3);
        (ii)~\emph{wall-clock forwarder hold/release} with real delays: within-horizon 180\,s
        holds seal and show multi-revision successors (2/2 pass); beyond-horizon attempts
        (930\,s hold-release; 1080\,s quiet wait) \emph{seal} but do \emph{not} emit
        LateEvidenceRecords under organic telemetry (2 sealed, 0/2 LateEvidence; new membership
        formed instead). Beyond-horizon localization is therefore supported by controlled inject,
        not by wall-clock beyond runs in this lab.
  \item \textbf{M7 supplemental boundary-stress cohort:} ten sealed live runs (5 attack-only +
        5 concurrent-benign) with ability offsets $0/300/3900$\,s. Production formation yields
        coverage $0$, F1 $0$, and undefined contamination under $W_{max}=3600$\,s. M7 is a
        descriptive long-horizon stress cell and is excluded from primary cell/mode aggregates.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

Two Ubuntu targets; non-commercial EDR; $n=5$ per cell; controlled benign workloads; no
production base rates; fair baselines tied on primary M1--M6; synthetic cards $\neq$ field
validation; raw order not robust; no fitted scorer/calibration; no partial-telemetry/outage;
M2 mapping not corrected in the primary cohort. Organic hub stress remains unobserved on live
streams. Wall-clock \emph{within}-horizon holds are executed (2/2); wall-clock
\emph{beyond}-horizon LateEvidenceRecord under organic telemetry was attempted (2 sealed) but
not observed. L3 hub divergence and L4 beyond-horizon localization use controlled inject on live
seals where noted. M7 is supplemental only.

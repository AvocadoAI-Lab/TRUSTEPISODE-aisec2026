\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

The evaluation is a frozen protocol, not a report of completed experiments. Populations,
splits, manifests, thresholds, gates, interventions, matching, metrics, resampling, and empty
RT1--RT7 schemas are fixed before locked-test access. Every unexecuted cell is
\texttt{pending}; no synthetic value, curve, or interval is reported.

\subsection{Research Questions and Populations}
\label{sec:research-questions}

\textbf{RQ1 -- formation.} Does the label-blind builder increase malicious-action coverage
over time-only and time-plus-entity comparators while reducing fragmentation and over-merge?
\textbf{RQ2 -- ranking/calibration.} Does $D+C$ improve AUPRC and fixed-budget recall over
severity and $D$-only comparators, and does independent affine calibration improve Brier, NLL,
and reliability? \textbf{RQ3 -- partial telemetry.} Under paired replay and separate physical
outages, how do formation, $z$, support, $U$, revisions, and eligibility change?
\textbf{RQ4 -- replay/cost.} Are canonical outputs byte identical, and what throughput,
latency, memory, storage, and replay time are measured?

The populations are disjoint views of the same registered runs. $\mathcal C_{form}$ contains
one terminal revision per surviving episode lineage at
$T_{form}=t_{run\_end}+L_{wm}+W_{rev}=t_{run\_end}+1020$ s; merged parents are removed.
$\mathcal C_{dec}^{all}$ contains the AssemblyOutcome of the first \texttt{finalized} revision
per terminal lineage, including supported/OOS AuditRecord and AssemblyFailureRecord.
The scorable inventory is
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C_{dec}^{score}=\{o\in\mathcal C_{dec}^{all}:&\ o\text{ is AuditRecord},\\
&o.decision\_eligible,\ P\in[0,1]\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
After every terminal outcome is
inserted, the offline harness joins label $L(o)\in\{positive,negative,mixed,ambiguous\}$ by its
EpisodeRevision digest, including for AssemblyFailureRecord. The binary metric population is
\begin{equation*}
\mathcal C_{dec}^{binary}=\{o\in\mathcal C_{dec}^{score}:L(o)\in\{positive,negative\}\}.
\end{equation*}
$\mathcal C_{stream}$ contains every
terminal AssemblyOutcome keyed by $(episode\_id,revision)$, including AssemblyFailureRecord, and
is used only for RQ3/RQ4 transition and cost analysis. Every partition/run reports
supported-global, supported-group, OOS-AuditRecord, and failure counts and rates with denominator
$|\mathcal C_{dec}^{all}|$; scorable coverage uses $|\mathcal C_{dec}^{score}|$, while every
class-dependent ranking, discrimination, and proper-score metric uses
$|\mathcal C_{dec}^{binary}|$. RT1 also reports outcome and failure counts by all four offline
labels, exposing class-dependent assembly failure.
Thus
late revisions cannot duplicate RQ1/RQ2 candidates and assembly failure cannot induce hidden
survivorship bias.

\subsection{Controlled Laboratory and Frozen Split}
\label{sec:datasets}

are related-work context, not an evaluation tier. Its four-service primary topology is Caldera

\subsection{Ground Truth, Matching, and Labels}
\label{sec:ground-truth}

Allowlist schemas reject \path{operation_id}, \path{ability_id}, adversary/technique labels,
action receipts/windows, scenario class, malicious label, and split assignment from every online
object~\cite{kaufman2012leakage}. Each ground-truth group $g$ registers a nonempty set of
required action tokens $A_g$; each run registers benign/background tokens $B_r$. For predicted
episode $p$, $A_{cov}(p,g)=|A_p\cap A_g|/|A_g|$, where $A_p$ is its root-deduplicated attributed
action set. Offline matching constructs the threshold-qualified edge graph $E_q$. An edge first
requires temporal IoU $\ge0.10$, entity Jaccard $\ge0.50$, and one exact offline action receipt or
registered action. Its weight is
\begin{equation}
w=0.5A_{cov}+0.3J_{entity}+0.2\operatorname{IoU}_{time};
\quad w\ge\theta_{match}=0.50.
\label{eq:match-weight}
\end{equation}
Maximum-weight one-to-one bipartite assignment over $E_q$ operates within one run; ties use
entity overlap, absolute time difference, then ascending ASCII/UTF-8 bytes of
$(episode\_id,group\_id)$. Assignment is used only for episode
precision/recall/F1. Coverage, fragmentation, and over-merge use unassigned $E_q$.

A revision chooses the first incident edge under descending $(A_{cov},w)$ then ascending UTF-8
group ID; with no edge, $g^*=\texttt{null}$, $A_{cov}=0$, and $B_{cont}$ is undefined. Otherwise contamination is
$B_{cont}=|A_p\cap B_r|/|(A_p\cap A_{g^*})\cup(A_p\cap B_r)|$; a zero denominator is undefined.
First-match label precedence is: mixed if $A_{cov}\ge\theta_{mal}=0.50$ and
$B_{cont}\ge\theta_{contam}=0.25$; positive if coverage passes and contamination is below the
threshold; negative if $A_{cov}=0$, at least one registered benign token is present, and no
conflict exists; otherwise ambiguous, including undefined contamination. A truth conflict exists
iff overlapping registered groups assign one root/action token to different group IDs, or the
same root is both malicious and benign, or duplicate receipt IDs disagree on card/window; any
conflict forces ambiguous before assignment. Labels are joined only after terminal
AssemblyOutcome insertion; the join resolves EpisodeRevision independently of whether assembly
succeeded, and an unresolved revision is counted as ambiguous. Sensitivity varies $\theta_{mal}\in\{.25,.50,.75\}$,
$\theta_{contam}\in\{.10,.25,.50\}$, and
$\theta_{match}\in\{.40,.50,.60\}$ without altering the primary registry. Figure
~\ref{fig:ground-truth-firewall} shows the enforced join boundary.

\subsection{Replay Perturbations and Physical Outage}
\label{sec:partial-telemetry}

The operators define deterministic children for every M1--M7 base run, but formal RT4 instantiates
only the 28 malicious locked-test roots (seven cards, two modes, two locked repetitions). Each
root has 41 RP0--RP7 children, for 1,148 sealed replay bundles; non-locked roots are never read by
RT4. RP2--RP7 instantiate the Cartesian product of source
$\in\{\mathrm{EDR},\mathrm{NDR}\}$ and registered dose; RP1 explicitly uses
EDR-only/NDR-only and RP0 has no source. Let
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
M&=base\|0x1f\|RP\|0x1f\|source\\
 &\quad\|0x1f\|JCS(level),\\
S&=\operatorname{HMAC}_{SHA256}(\texttt{20260710},M),\\
U(d,k)&=\operatorname{uint64be}\bigl(
\operatorname{HMAC}_{SHA256}(S,d\|0x1f\|k)[0{:}8]\bigr)\\
&\quad/2^{64}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
Base events sort by $(t^{event},t^{ingest},source\_instance,event\_id)$. RP0 is unchanged; RP1
masks one family; RP2 drops explicit-source events at 5/10/25\%; RP3 removes an explicit-source
30/120/300 s half-open interval; RP4 delays ingest by 30/120/600 s; RP5 reorders within
30/120/600 s buffers anchored at minimum base ingest time; RP6 shifts event time by
$-120/-30/+30/+120$ s; and RP7 duplicates explicit-source root groups at 5/10/25\%. Other-source
events and SourceCoverage remain unchanged.

RP3 uses $j=\lfloor U(\texttt{interval},\texttt{start})N\rfloor$ over $N$ ascending
integer-microsecond valid starts; $N=0$ invalidates the child. RP5 width is exactly the dose in
integer microseconds and its within-buffer key is $(U(\texttt{permute},source\|event\_id),
event\_id)$. RP7 preserves the root and sets the duplicate ID to SHA-256 of byte preimage
the concatenation of UTF-8 \texttt{RP7}, separator \texttt{0x1f}, raw $S_{32}$, another
separator, UTF-8 event ID, a final separator, and $\operatorname{uint32be}(1)$, with ingest
time increased by one microsecond; collision invalidates the child.

PO1 is a separate locked-test-only cohort, not a child of all 220 base runs. It expands
$\{\mathrm{EDR},\mathrm{NDR}\}\times\{120,600\}$ s $\times$ 10 repetitions into exactly 40
physical traces. With card order M1/M3/M4/M7, source index EDR/NDR $=0/1$, duration index
120/600 $=0/1$, and repetition $r\in[0,9]$, the card index is
$(r+2i_{source}+i_{duration})\bmod4$, assigning exactly 10 traces per card. TrustEpisode and OB1
reuse the same immutable trace and health journal. For each cell, $t_{anchor}$ begins after two
healthy heartbeats with valid parser, live transport, and backlog $\le120$ s. Continuous health
for 120 s gives $t_{stop}=t_{anchor}+120$ s. After authenticated collector stop, the allocated
card begins and at least one ability executes during the outage; the collector remains stopped
until $t_{restore}=t_{stop}+d$. The registry records $t_{detect}$, $t_{h1}$, $t_{h2}$, and first
$t_{ready}$ beginning two consecutive parser-valid, live, backlog-$\le120$ heartbeat intervals;
$t_{stable}\ge\max(t_{h2},t_{ready})$ is followed by 900 s without a revision. Detection is
right-censored at restore if absent; recovery is right-censored at
$t_{censor}=t_{restore}+1800$ s if ready/stable is absent. Uncensored durations are
$t_{detect}-t_{stop}$ and $t_{stable}-t_{restore}$. Each PO1 trace is its own bootstrap cluster
and is excluded from fitting and base-run RT2/RT3 populations.

\subsection{Metrics and Run-Level Uncertainty}
\label{sec:metrics}

For run $r$, let $E_{q,r}$ be its unassigned threshold-qualified graph. For each ground-truth
group $g$, $n_g=|\{p:(p,g)\in E_{q,r}\}|$; for episode $p$,
$d_p=|\{g:(p,g)\in E_{q,r}\}|$. RQ1 uses run-macro action coverage,
$|G_r|^{-1}\sum_g\max(0,n_g-1)$ fragmentation, and
$|P_r|^{-1}\sum_p\mathbf{1}[d_p>1]$ over-merge. Run action coverage is
$|G_r|^{-1}\sum_g\max_p A_{cov}(p,g)$; run contamination is the arithmetic mean over defined
$B_{cont}$ values in $\mathcal C_{form,r}$, with undefined count reported separately. Empty
$G_r$ or $P_r$ makes the corresponding metric undefined, never zero. One-to-one assignment is
used only for episode F1, so neither graph metric is forced to zero.

RQ2 uses $\mathcal C_{dec}^{binary}$ only: AUPRC and recall among the top
$\max(1,\lceil0.10|\mathcal C_{dec,r}^{binary}|\rceil)$ per run are primary ranking metrics; Brier, NLL,
and ten-bin reliability use that same positive/negative population only. AUROC and precision@$k$ are secondary
~\cite{saito2015pr}. RQ3 reports paired run-macro changes and all $U$ transitions; RT5 reports
outage detection/recovery. RQ4 checks byte equality and measured cost. RT6 replays frozen RP0
inputs at worker counts 1/2/4/8 on the manifest-pinned host, with one excluded warm-up and five
measured repeats per run/worker. A monotonic clock measures ingest-to-AssemblyOutcome latency;
RSS is sampled every 100 ms; storage sums canonical bytes; throughput includes all terminal
outcomes, and each row records hardware/software manifest digest and failure fraction.

All primary summaries are macro-averaged over runs; pooled micro results are supplemental.
Intervals use 2,000 percentile bootstrap resamples of complete run clusters with seed 20260710
~\cite{field2007bootstrap}; replay/outage resample base-run pairs. Undefined class-dependent
resamples are counted and omitted, never zeroed. Relative effects are supplemental.

\subsection{Limitations and Safety}
\label{sec:discussion}

The controlled lab cannot establish field generalization or every evasion/base rate.
Calibration applies only to exact supported manifests. Identity remains offline. Formal runs
use isolated hosts, test accounts, offline-only action receipts, cleanup gates, and recorded failures. The
evaluation does not study CTI, agents, impact, priority, OT/ICS, or autonomous response, and
reports no empirical result until the locked pipeline executes.

\title{TrustEpisode: Auditable Evidence Contracts for AI-Assisted EDR/NDR Investigation}

\author{Anonymous Authors}
\affiliation{\institution{Anonymous Institution}}
\email{anonymous@anonymous.edu}

\begin{abstract}
AI-assisted security investigation turns sparse tool outputs into confident narratives, yet
arrival order, collector failure, and late evidence can silently alter the facts exposed to an
analyst or agent. We present \trustepisode{}, a deterministic (over canonicalized, version-bound
inputs), auditable EDR/NDR episode-reconstruction contract. Episode Synthesis alone owns membership;
revisions and terminal audit outcomes are append-only; source health differs from ``no detection;''
unsupported probabilities remain null; and downstream AI reads versioned evidence without authority
over case facts.

On 70 primary malicious formation runs (40 standalone benign controls are not detection trials),
the sealed cohort shows a descriptive mean coverage increase from \EbZeroCov{} to \TeCov{} relative
to the legacy fixed-bin reference. Fair sliding baselines show no superiority: EB4 ties EB2 on all
action-coverage pairs (\EbTwoActionWTL{} W/T/L) and trails EB1 on one concurrent-benign M7 run
(\EbOneActionWTL{}; mean $\Delta=\EbOneActionDelta{}$, 95\% CI
$[\EbOneActionCILo{},\EbOneActionCIHi{}]$). Canonical replay is byte-identical on \DetNRuns{}
M1--M6 runs, whereas raw arrival order is not robust without re-canonicalization.
\AuditPass{}/\AuditTotal{} audit fixtures cover seven terminal failure codes; an A--G
taxonomy localizes M2's repeatable gap to class D (token mapping failure); and a deployed case trace preserves one
Suricata and two Wazuh events with ATT\&CK references.

All \AgentBoundaryPass{}/\AgentBoundaryTotal{} synthetic boundary cards behave as
specified: the valid control is accepted, six invalid or unsealed mutations are rejected, and a
resealed allowed-field mutation is accepted, showing digest integrity still requires external
writer authorization. A once-run held-out 20-card Qwen2.5-7B pilot yields
\LlmPilotExact{}/\LlmPilotTotal{} fully exact responses; locked same-card replication on
deployment-default Gemma3-1B yields \GemmaPilotExact{}/20. These audited configurations support no
general LLM-accuracy or fitted-calibration claim; typed evidence instead makes their errors reviewable.
\end{abstract}
